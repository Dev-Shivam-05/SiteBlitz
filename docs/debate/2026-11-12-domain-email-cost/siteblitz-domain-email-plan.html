Two .in rules to know:
1. Identity check (KYC) is compulsory. After buying, the registrar asks us to verify identity within a few days. If we skip it, the domain can be suspended.
2. "Hide my name" services are not allowed for .in. We must give our real name and address. This is fine: the registry's public lookup already hides the owner's name, email and street address (checked on existing .in domains). Only the state and country show.

2. Business email: Google Workspace (this is "business Gmail")

  • siteblitz.in@gmail.com is not business email. It is just a normal free Gmail ID; customers would still see "@gmail.com".
  • Real business Gmail = Google Workspace. We get the normal Gmail app and inbox, but the address is hello@siteblitz.in.

3. Extra addresses (aliases): yes, free

  • All of them land in the same inbox, and we can reply from any of them.

5. Options we checked and rejected

  • Free trick: forward email to personal Gmail and reply "as" hello@siteblitz.in. Many YouTube and blog guides teach this. Google is ending it in January 2027 for non-Google addresses (official Google help page). Not worth setting up now.
  • Forwarding + a separate sending service + a desktop mail app. Free and technically works, but it is two systems to maintain, and it is easy to reply from a personal address by mistake.
  • Buying the domain abroad (Porkbun etc.). Slightly cheaper renewals, but billed in dollars with card forex charges. Their free "hide my name" feature can't be used for .in anyway.
  • Cloudflare. It does not sell .in domains.

6. Steps and who does what

  1. Buy siteblitz.in on Hostinger India, 3-year term (check the cart total is about ₹2,123). Real name and address. Finish the KYC when asked.
  2. Start Google Workspace Business Base, choose "I have a domain", create hello@siteblitz.in.
  3. Copy the records Google shows into Hostinger's DNS settings: ownership check, mail delivery (MX), and three anti-spam records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
  4. Add aliases: Admin console → Directory → Users → hello → Add Alternate Emails.
  5. Send and receive one real test email (to a Gmail and an Outlook address). Then Claude switches the email address on in the website.

Steps 1-4 need payment and logins, so we do them; step 5's code change is done by Claude.
