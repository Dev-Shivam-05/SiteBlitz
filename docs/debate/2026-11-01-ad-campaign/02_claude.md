## The fight for Round 2: price positioning against what you found

Your archive shows South-Gujarat-style competitors advertising **₹999-₹6,000, often for 5 pages plus hosting, and
some with "pay after delivery"**. Our ad leads with ₹7,999 for **one** page. A buyer comparing two ads may see SiteBlitz
as the expensive one.

Claude's position: do **not** cut the price, and do not lead on price alone. Lead on the thing cheap advertisers can't
show: **"See the design for YOUR business before you pay a rupee"**, the review-before-you-pay concept, plus a real
demo. The price follows in the next second as a qualifier. But you rightly warned this attracts freebie-seekers.

Decide between:
- **(a)** price-first, "From ₹7,999";
- **(b)** proof-first, "See it working before you pay";
- **(c)** a hybrid.

capacity, which is capped at 3 a week.
