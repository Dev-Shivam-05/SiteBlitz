Operating rule: No contact without a lawful, appropriate channel; no invented buying intent; no automated initial outreach; no paid campaign without an approved budget.

The first acquisition system needs to answer four questions:

- Can SiteBlitz find businesses with a real, relevant problem?
- Can it reach their decision-makers appropriately?
- Will those decision-makers consider paying to solve the problem?
- Can SiteBlitz acquire and serve them profitably?

These questions matter more than the quantity of prospects collected.

### Four important platform and legal findings

1\. Google Business Profile is not available to every online studio.

Google states that eligible businesses generally need in-person contact with customers during stated hours. Online-only businesses and lead-generation businesses are ineligible. Virtual offices do not qualify simply because the company can receive mail there. Genuine service-area businesses can be eligible under specific rules.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://support.google.com\&sz=32)

Google Business Profile Help+1



Decision: Do not create or claim a local profile until the founder confirms how and where SiteBlitz actually meets customers. A verified physical or service-area business is not equivalent to a remote-only software agency.

2\. Storing a Google Place ID is allowed, but that does not authorize a Google-powered prospecting database.

The current Places API documentation permits storing `place_id` values indefinitely and recommends refreshing IDs older than 12 months. However, the broader Maps Platform Terms prohibit extracting or saving Maps content such as business names and addresses for use outside the licensed service. The specific application purpose, permitted display, attribution and storage conditions still matter.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://developers.google.com\&sz=32)

Google for Developers+2



Decision: Keep Google Places automation in a blocked, research-only backlog until the proposed prospecting use case is explicitly reviewed against the relevant license. A permitted persistent ID is not a loophole for copying the associated business data into CRM.

3\. A published business email address is not automatically permission to send marketing.

India's telecom unsolicited-commercial-communication framework principally governs commercial messages and calls through telecom channels; it should not be presented as a blanket statutory prohibition on all business email, nor as proof that every unsolicited business email is lawful.

The November 13, 2025 DPDP commencement notification also stages different provisions across immediate, one-year and eighteen-month effective dates. The main processing provisions are not all in force as of November 1, 2026. That does not eliminate obligations under other applicable laws or sender policies.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://www.trai.gov.in\&sz=32)

Government of India+2



Google's sender guidance recommends sending marketing to people who want it, using legitimate sender authentication and offering straightforward unsubscribe mechanisms.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://support.google.com\&sz=32)

Gmail Help



Decision: The proposed ten-emails-per-day limit is a voluntary operating cap, not a legal safe harbor. Keep unsolicited email on hold until the founder's actual process, recipient type, jurisdiction, permission basis and platform rules are reviewed. Permissioned introductions, responses to genuine inquiries and legitimate existing relationships are preferable.

4\. Referral fees need profession-specific review.

Disclosing a referral fee is not sufficient by itself. The ICAI's revised Code of Ethics applies from April 1, 2026 and contains professional conduct restrictions that need checking before offering payments to chartered accountants. Other regulated professions can present similar risks.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://ethics.icai.org\&sz=32)

ethics.icai.org



Decision: Start with unpaid introductions and transparent partnerships. Any commercial referral agreement must be reviewed for the partner's professional restrictions, conflicts of interest and applicable disclosure obligations.

### Cross-channel economic rule

Use the same denominator everywhere:

\\[ \text{CAC}=\frac{\text{ad + fees + attributable sales labor + other acquisition costs}}{\text{new paying customers}} \\]

Where no customers have paid, CAC is undefined, not ₹0.

Once deliveries occur:

\\[ \text{Acquisition-adjusted contribution} =\text{net project revenue}-\text{delivery costs}-\text{acquisition costs} \\]

Neither the ₹17,999 price nor a cheap enquiry guarantees positive contribution.

## Q4.3 — Minimum viable lead-discovery system

### C17 verdict: AGREE with semi-manual discovery and rule-based scoring, but change the architecture in four places

Claude proposes a `Prospect` record with source evidence, website observations, reviewer notes and a 100-point score.

This is directionally right.

My corrections are:

First: Distinguish factual observations from commercial hypotheses at the data-model level—not just in text labels.

Second: A score cannot authorize outreach. Legal contact eligibility must be an independent gate.

Third: A missing website and a broken website are different types of evidence. Do not claim "no website" solely because a particular search failed.

Fourth: The system needs controlled source retention and revalidation. Stored screenshots and extracted pages can become inaccurate, unnecessary or subject to third-party restrictions.

### Proposed discovery and qualification flow

1\. Independent prospect identification

Official business website · approved directory · permitted introduction

2\. Source and identity verification

Company, website, locality, duplicate detection, provenance

3\. Permitted website assessment

Bounded checks, timestamped evidence, no intrusive testing

4\. Explainable fit score and human review

Business fit ≠ website defect ≠ interest in purchasing

5\. Permission and suppression gate

Eligible contact channel · no opt-out · founder approval

6\. Actual human response

Verified interest → qualified lead → proposal → payment

No step should automatically initiate contact merely because a prospect's score exceeds a threshold.

### Minimum data model

| Entity               | Essential fields                                                                     | Purpose                                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Prospect`           | Business identity, sector, locality, normalized official domain, review status       | One canonical company record                              |
| `SourceEvidence`     | Field, value, source URL, access date, evidence type, reviewer                       | Trace where each fact came from                           |
| `WebsiteObservation` | URL, issue category, observed behavior, test conditions, timestamp, confidence       | Reproducible website findings                             |
| `ContactMethod`      | Published general address or permitted introduction, source, verification state      | Reachability without unnecessary personal-data collection |
| `ContactEligibility` | Channel, applicable restrictions, permission evidence, reviewer, expiry/recheck date | Separate legal and policy gate                            |
| `Review`             | Fit score, observations, reviewer, uncertainty and recommended next action           | Explainable prioritization                                |
| `OutreachEvent`      | Approval, channel, message reference, timestamp, outcome                             | Accountability                                            |
| `Suppression`        | Minimal business/contact identifiers, scope, reason and date                         | Prevent unwanted future contact                           |
| `Lead`               | Linked prospect, actual inquiry or response, requirements, owner                     | Human-confirmed commercial relationship                   |
| `Opportunity`        | Agreed scope, proposal, value, stage, payment and attribution                        | Financial outcome                                         |

For the first 30 businesses, these records can initially be managed in a small, access-controlled table or the focused admin interface. A complex enrichment platform is unnecessary.

### Scoring rubric — 100 points, explicitly not buying intent

I accept C17's 40/40/20 model with these refinements.

| Dimension                            | Proposed score | Evidence                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Segment fit                          | 0–20           | Coaching/clinic match against approved criteria                                       |
| Locality and serviceability          | 0–10           | Founder-operable area or suitable remote fit                                          |
| Independent decision-maker structure | 0–10           | Evidence of independent ownership versus chain-level purchasing                       |
| Verified enquiry-path problem        | 0–15           | Observable missing or inaccessible contact path; no unauthorized form submission      |
| Verified mobile usability problem    | 0–10           | Repeatable narrow-screen issue                                                        |
| Verified technical problem           | 0–10           | Publicly observable serious HTTPS or availability fault                               |
| Verified clarity/accessibility issue | 0–5            | Documented problem affecting a normal visitor                                         |
| Contactability                       | 0–20           | Verified, appropriate channel, with higher scores only when permission is established |
| Maximum                              | 100            | Score must show component evidence                                                    |

Contactability without permission should remain blocked even if the numeric score is high.

To avoid gaming the model, overlapping manifestations of the same underlying issue should not accumulate separate scores without justification. Every rule needs a definition, evidence link and manual override history.

An important correction: "No website found" should initially be recorded as website not located after specified searches, not as a proven defect. An absent site cannot be audited like a broken existing homepage. If the business confirms it has no website, that creates stronger evidence, but still not buying intent.

### Three evidence classifications

| Classification      | Meaning                                                                        | Example                                     | May initiate sales automatically?              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `OBSERVED_DEFECT`   | Directly reproduced website problem                                            | Contact link returns 404                    | No                                             |
| `INFERRED_NEED`     | A hypothesis about a possible commercial problem                               | Better enquiry handling may help this owner | No                                             |
| `VERIFIED_INTEREST` | An authorized human expresses interest in discussing or purchasing the service | Owner asks for a proposal                   | No automatic sale; human qualification follows |

These are not necessarily sequential statuses. A business can express interest without any observed defect. A business can also have severe defects and no interest in purchasing anything.

Never infer verified interest from email opens, website visits, a high grader score, a social-media interaction or the presence of an email address.

### Grader validation: accept Claude's 15-site test

The grader should be evaluated against 15 appropriately selected public websites, including both target segments and sites with different technologies and quality levels.

For each observation, record what the tool reported, what an independent reviewer confirmed, whether the observation is reproducible and whether the business implication is supported.

The key metric is:

\\[ \text{False-positive rate} =\frac{\text{incorrect defect findings}}{\text{all defect findings reviewed}} \\]

Also record false negatives, inconclusive results and assessments that cannot be performed without permission. Avoid inventing an accuracy rate before the test.

### Website-assessment safeguards

The current grader reportedly includes an SSRF guard. Claude should verify its actual behavior under redirect chains and DNS changes before extending it into prospect assessment.

Checks should use bounded timeouts, resource limits and permitted public pages only. Respect applicable site terms, robots directives and access restrictions. Do not bypass authentication, test private endpoints or submit forms that could create real customer records without authorization.

A `robots.txt` allowance is not, by itself, permission for every automated use. Google's own documentation explains that robots directives govern crawler behavior rather than enforcing access authorization.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://developers.google.com\&sz=32)

Google for Developers



Store only evidence necessary for review. Screenshots should be access-controlled, retained for a documented period and not republished as if SiteBlitz owns the target website.

### Deduplication correction

Claude's proposed domain → phone → name-plus-locality sequence is useful, but these should be matching signals rather than unconditional merge rules.

A parent company can operate multiple independent branches. Several locations can share one phone number, and unrelated firms may share third-party booking or agency domains.

Normalize domains and phone numbers, generate potential matches, then require human review for ambiguous records. Preserve merge history and allow correction.

### Build, buy or manually operate?

| ---------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Manual review with a small table   | Low                 | Fast learning, source accuracy    | NOW                                                |
| Rule-based prioritization in admin | Low–medium          | Explainable, repeatable selection | NEXT                                               |

## Q4.4 — Permission, suppression, outreach control and attribution

### C18 verdict: AGREE; suppression must be a hard operational gate

The proposed suppression list should apply across email, phone, WhatsApp and partner-generated outreach, subject to the specific scope of the person's or company's request.

### Suppression workflow

Before every proposed contact

| 1. Identity match    | Compare domain, business identity and relevant contact identifiers     |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2. Suppression check | Block prohibited, opted-out, bounced or complained-about recipients    |
| 3. Channel check     | Verify appropriate permission and applicable provider rules            |
| 4. Human approval    | Record approving person, proposed message and reason                   |
| 5. Delivery/outcome  | Record actual sending result, reply, bounce, complaint and any opt-out |

A business-wide opt-out should suppress all promotional contacts with that business. If the wording only concerns one personal address, preserve the request's actual scope and avoid contacting another employee merely to circumvent it.

Existing customers may still need necessary service or transaction communications, but those must not be used to disguise marketing.

One opt-out ends the relevant promotional sequence immediately. Do not require another founder approval to honor a suppression request.

### Required audit record

Each approved outreach event needs the canonical prospect ID, permitted channel, contact source, approval identity and timestamp, version of the message, actual sending status and subsequent outcome.

Avoid storing unnecessary personal details or unlimited raw content. Use controlled retention and access permissions. The legal basis and retention policy require review before production use.

The 2025 DPDP notification sets phased commencement, with one group of provisions coming into force one year after publication and major data-processing provisions after eighteen months. SiteBlitz should prepare for the relevant upcoming requirements rather than incorrectly claiming the entire framework has either already commenced or does not matter.&#x20;

### C19 verdict: AGREE with channel-level UTMs, but require explicit attribution uncertainty

UTM parameters are useful for online acquisition. They do not fully capture an offline buyer journey.

A prospective client might first hear about SiteBlitz through a printer, inspect its website directly three days later and finally send a WhatsApp message. Assigning that sale entirely to direct website traffic would be misleading.

### Proposed attribution records

| Field                     | What it answers                                         |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `first_known_source`      | Where was the first attributable interaction?           |
| `last_meaningful_source`  | What channel directly preceded qualification?           |
| `self_reported_source`    | How does the buyer say they heard about SiteBlitz?      |
| `campaign_id`             | Which experiment was active?                            |
| `source_confidence`       | Is attribution confirmed, inferred or unknown?          |
| `proposal_value`          | What was quoted?                                        |
| `payment_received`        | What revenue actually arrived?                          |
| `acquisition_time`        | How much founder effort was used?                       |
| `direct_acquisition_cost` | What advertising, referral or other fees were incurred? |

UTM values should identify channels and campaigns, not people.

For example:

`utm_source=partner&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=clinic_pilot`

Do not place a recipient's name, email, phone number or other personal identifier in URL parameters.

## Stress test: Could the acquisition plan itself become a distraction?

The most serious risk in Claude's C17 proposal is premature automation.

SiteBlitz could spend substantial time building prospect tables, scoring systems, screenshot capture, integrations and enrichment workflows without speaking to a single buyer.

Therefore, I recommend this strict development boundary:

| Stage                       | Allowed capability                                                | Unlock condition for next stage                                     |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First 30 prospects          | Manual research, verified sources, simple records and suppression | Relevant conversations and actual qualification evidence            |

## Round 4 conclusion — Handoff to Claude Code

I accept Claude's fundamental approach: founder-led customer discovery, carefully evidenced website assessments, manual approval and simple explainable prospect scoring.

I challenge three assumptions that remain consequential:

C16: Published contact details do not automatically grant marketing permission, and disclosed referral payments are not automatically professional-ethics compliant.

C17: Google Places storage allowances cannot be treated as general permission to build a prospect database. Also, a website defect is not a verified need or buying signal.

C19: Channel-level UTM tracking is useful but insufficient for an offline relationship-led sales process. Actual qualification and payment records must complete the attribution chain.

### Recommended first executable acquisition package

Before implementing an elaborate lead engine, SiteBlitz needs a working contact system, two equivalent niche pages, one honestly labeled demonstration, a small provenance-aware prospect register, a suppression mechanism and a reviewable sales process.

That is sufficient to conduct the 15/15 experiment without inventing intent, breaking platform rules or committing advertising expenditure.

Round 4 disposition: Substantial agreement on channel sequencing, semi-manual discovery and human oversight. Email eligibility, paid partnerships and Places API prospecting require additional verification. The exact scoring model remains experimental.

Proceed to Round 5 — Founder-operated admin, CRM, APIs and technical feasibility. The central question for Claude should be whether SiteBlitz can manage a complete customer lifecycle with a simple two-person system, while deferring advanced ad management and automation until they are commercially justified.
