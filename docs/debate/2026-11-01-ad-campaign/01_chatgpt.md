### 1.2 Important finding: Meta's lead optimization

Meta offers more than simple conversation optimization for eligible click-to-WhatsApp advertisers.

Its published research found that optimizing click-to-WhatsApp ads for leads produced, on average, 24% lower cost per lead than optimizing for conversations. This was a cross-advertiser experiment in November 2024, not a SiteBlitz forecast. Eligibility and conversion-event requirements must be checked in the actual account.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://whatsappbusiness.com\&sz=32)

WhatsApp for Business+2



My preferred setup hierarchy is:

1. First preference: Leads objective → WhatsApp messaging destination → maximize leads through messaging, provided the account is eligible and the required lead events can be supplied reliably.
2. Practical new-account fallback: WhatsApp destination → maximize conversations, using the supported Leads or Engagement setup shown in Ads Manager.
3. Avoid: optimizing solely for link clicks, reach or video views when the commercial goal is a paying customer.

Even a qualified lead is not a purchase. Without enough verified lead or purchase events, choosing a deeper optimization objective can provide an insufficient learning signal.

## 5. How to attract paying buyers rather than freebie-seekers

### 5.1 Lead-quality controls

| Stage            | Filter                                  | Purpose                                          |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Ad               | ₹7,999 starting price visible           | Screens out fundamental price mismatch           |
| Ad               | Explicit one-page website scope         | Prevents assumption of a large ecommerce website |
| Ad               | “For business owners”                   | Reduces irrelevant interest                      |
| WhatsApp opening | Business type and city                  | Establishes identity and service fit             |
| Qualification    | Desired website outcome                 | Confirms a real need                             |
| Qualification    | Decision authority and intended timing  | Establishes buying readiness                     |
| Proposal         | Exact scope, payment and revision terms | Prevents disputes and wasted delivery time       |

Recommendation: The free personalized homepage concept should not be the main ad headline. Otherwise the campaign risks attracting people who only want free design work.

Use the concept as a selective sales tool after qualification, capped at the founder-stated three concepts per week or nine total work-hours.

### 5.2 WhatsApp conversation after an ad reply

The opening response should be short, natural and relevant. It should acknowledge the specific demo or offer, then ask three questions:

1. What kind of business do you operate, and what should the website help customers do?
2. Are you the decision-maker, and are you considering the ₹7,999 one-page package or a larger project?
3. When would you want to start, assuming the scope and quotation work for you?

After a qualified reply, send the relevant demo, explain what it demonstrates and what it does not, then offer a short discussion or written proposal.

Important: Do not pretend the demo is a paying customer project. Do not promise increased sales, guaranteed Google rankings, or an arbitrary return on investment.

The eventual CRM should record where the buyer came from, which ad they saw, their interest, qualification, next action and whether a deposit was received.

## 6. Measurement, decision rules and the ₹800 stop conditions

### 6.1 The actual conversion funnel

Ad impressions

Visibility, not intent

WhatsApp conversations started

Actual customer-initiated chats

Qualified conversations

Owner, relevant need, price and timing

Proposals sent

Commercial opportunity

Payment received

First paying customer

North star

A paying customer means a genuine received payment or confirmed deposit, not a WhatsApp message saying “interested.”

### 6.2 Daily dashboard

Record spend, impressions, reach, link clicks, messaging conversations, cost per conversation, unique qualified chats, first-response time, proposals, deposits, collected revenue and reason for losing each lead.

Suggested formulas:

\\[ \text{Cost per qualified lead}=\frac{\text{Ad spend}}{\text{Qualified leads}} \\]

\\[ \text{Chat qualification rate}=\frac{\text{Qualified chats}}{\text{Total chats}}\times100 \\]

\\[ \text{Customer acquisition cost}=\frac{\text{Ad spend + applicable acquisition costs}}{\text{Paying customers}} \\]

For zero qualified leads or zero paying customers, the corresponding cost ratio is not yet a finite observed number. Report “no results” rather than inventing a useful cost estimate.

### 6.3 Decision rules

| Cumulative media spend | Action                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ₹0                     | Verify correct links, working WhatsApp, truthful offer, creative approval, billing cap and demo availability                                        |
| ₹150–₹200              | Check delivery and technical health; do not declare a performance winner                                                                            |
| ₹350–₹400              | Review actual conversations and qualification reasons; diagnose zero response or irrelevant enquiries                                               |
| ₹550–₹600              | If there are no qualified conversations, consider stopping and preserving the balance; if there is a promising conversation, continue under the cap |
| ₹800                   | Stop paid delivery and evaluate the full customer funnel                                                                                            |

These are proposed operational checkpoints, not statistically derived performance thresholds.

Immediate pause conditions should include a broken WhatsApp destination, misleading ad copy, unexpected billing, unapproved pricing, or the absence of the working demo advertised.

At the end of the campaign, distinguish three outcomes:

- One paying customer: Record revenue and fulfillment costs, then assess whether economics support another experiment. Do not immediately multiply spending.
- Qualified prospects, no payments: Diagnose consultation quality, offer fit, price objections, trust and follow-up before blaming the advertising creative.
- No qualified prospects: Investigate the creative, channel, audience and offer. This is one tiny inconclusive experiment, not proof that Meta cannot acquire customers.

For a ₹7,999 Launch sale and a ₹944 media-plus-estimated-GST experiment, approximately ₹7,055 remains before delivery labor, hosting, payment fees, taxes and other operating costs. It is not profit.
