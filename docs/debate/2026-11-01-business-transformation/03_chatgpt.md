Keep the energetic sense of confidence, disciplined typography, consistent calls to action and strong mobile attention.

Remove the WebGL smoke, repeated marquees, preloader and oversized outline typography from the primary buyer journeys. The desktop hamburger-only navigation should also be replaced with visible links where space permits.

This is not because dark themes or animations are inherently poor. It is because these specific effects currently contribute less to buyer confidence than a real demonstration would.

There are two legitimate implementation approaches:

Simulation: An interactive explanation uses fixed sample data and clearly says "No actual enquiry or message has been sent." It is a usable demonstration of the intended experience, not evidence of backend reliability.

Sandbox-backed live test: Uses an isolated non-production database and controlled notifications. This is stronger evidence, but requires permission, test identities, abuse controls, privacy review and verified delivery logs.

My recommendation: Start with an explicitly labeled simulation while independently testing the real lead flow. Upgrade to a controlled live demo only if the commercial benefit warrants the additional operational cost.

Do not permit an anonymous visitor to generate unlimited real notifications to the founder. Do not collect genuine student or patient details in a public demonstration.

## Q3.4 — Booking, contact, accessibility and founder-admin experience

### C14 verdict: AGREE, with two safeguards

"Request a call" is the correct terminology until the system verifies availability and confirms the appointment.

However, receiving a request, delivering an email receipt and actually reserving a meeting are three different events. The interface must not conflate them.

### Required contact and booking states

| State                 | Customer-visible message                                                             | Technical evidence required                           |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Validating            | "Checking your details…"                                                             | Client/server validation underway                     |
| Request saved         | "We've received your request. Reference: [ID]."                                      | Confirmed database write                              |
| Alert pending         | No claim that a human has read the request                                           | Persisted notification job and status                 |
| Human response        | "We'll respond during [approved business hours] using your selected contact method." | Founder-approved service commitment                   |
| Appointment requested | "Your preferred time has been noted. It isn't confirmed yet."                        | Request stored with proposed date/time                |
| Appointment confirmed | Exact date, time, timezone and confirmation method                                   | Human confirmation or genuine provider-backed booking |
| Submission failed     | "We couldn't save your request. You can retry or contact us directly."               | Clear failure result; user-activated fallback         |

A confirmation reference should be unpredictable and safe to disclose, not a sequential database identifier.

Email receipts: AGREE conditionally. Enable them only after SiteBlitz has a verified sender domain and tested transactional email service. A provider accepting an email for delivery does not establish that the recipient received it. Do not display a false "email delivered" message.

Calendar: AGREE that a custom scheduling integration is NEXT, not NOW. But a verified, provider-hosted appointment page can be a lower-effort alternative when the founder is ready. Google Calendar's appointment schedules can expose available slots and add confirmed appointments to a calendar.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://support.google.com\&sz=32)

Google Calendar Help



The system must have one authoritative booking mechanism, including timezone and conflict handling. It should never duplicate appointment confirmation across a disconnected custom form and an external calendar.

### Alternative and trade-off

Alternative: Implement full calendar synchronization, reminders and rescheduling before launching the new landing pages.

Why it loses: A manually confirmed call request is an acceptable first-sales process if communicated accurately. Comprehensive scheduling is unnecessary before consultation volume justifies it.

Trade-off: Manual confirmation requires founder time but avoids building and maintaining integrations before demand is established.

Validation: Submit a request, verify saved status and notification, confirm manually, and test duplicate requests and unconfirmed time selections.

Confidence: HIGH.

## C15 — UX acceptance criteria

### Verdict: AGREE with the proposed tests; expand them into measurable release gates

The five-second test, 360-pixel viewport rule, form-completion target and Core Web Vitals thresholds are appropriate starting points.

However, a five-person study is qualitative evidence, not statistical proof that a design increases conversion. Also, the proposed "no admin page without an action" rule is too absolute: read-only evidence and audit pages can be valuable when they support a decision.

### Public website acceptance matrix

| Category                  | Proposed pass condition                                                                                                                    | Verification                                            |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Five-second comprehension | At least 4 of 5 coaching owners and 4 of 5 clinic owners independently identify the service, intended buyer and approximate starting price | Unprompted recall; record wrong answers and hesitations |
| Mobile first screens      | At 360 px, offer, price and main CTA are visible within two ordinary mobile viewports; preferably the first                                | Actual screenshots at 320/360/390 px                    |
| Form time                 | At least 4 of 5 target participants complete a realistic enquiry in 60 seconds or less without assistance                                  | Timed user tests; record abandonments and reasons       |
| Form recovery             | Invalid input, lost connection and backend failure preserve entered information and show clear next steps                                  | Automated plus real-device failure tests                |
| Keyboard navigation       | All required functions work with keyboard alone; focus is visible; focus order is logical                                                  | Manual keyboard walkthrough                             |
| Screen-reader support     | Inputs have labels; state changes and errors are announced meaningfully                                                                    | NVDA or VoiceOver walkthrough                           |
| Contrast                  | WCAG 2.2 AA: at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for qualifying large text                                                              | Automated contrast checks plus manual review            |
| Touch targets             | Prefer approximately 44×44 CSS px interactive targets, while satisfying applicable WCAG 2.2 requirements                                   | Mobile inspection                                       |
| Reflow                    | No content loss or unnecessary horizontal scrolling at 320 CSS px equivalent width                                                         | Narrow-screen and zoom testing                          |
| Reduced motion            | No required function depends on animations, and reduced-motion preference is respected                                                     | Browser preference tests                                |
| Demo integrity            | Simulated events are identified as simulated; no production data contamination                                                             | Isolated demo tests and database inspection             |
| SEO and accessibility     | Semantic headings, crawlable content, appropriate metadata and usable navigation                                                           | Source review, automated checks and manual QA           |

W3C specifies a 4.5:1 normal-text contrast minimum at AA, error identification and suggestions, and content reflow requirements. WCAG 2.2's minimum pointer target requirement is generally 24×24 CSS pixels, subject to listed exceptions; recommending larger touch targets is a separate usability preference.&#x20;

Every mutable action should show a real success, failure or pending status. The UI must not optimistically claim that a lead was updated when the server rejected the change.

## Round 3 conclusion — Handoff to Claude Code

The new SiteBlitz must not simply look more expensive or modern. It must make the actual service easier to understand, inspect, purchase and trust.

My recommended first deliverable is a single coherent buyer-facing experience, comprising a mobile-first offer, a working but explicitly labeled concept, transparent pricing, a real contact path and visible human accountability.

The coaching and clinic variants should share this foundation while the founder's 15/15 experiment determines the stronger initial niche.

The critical distinction for Round 3 is between showing something impressive and demonstrating something real. SiteBlitz needs the latter.

Round 3 disposition: Substantial agreement on A+B, staged delivery, honest proof and booking semantics. Founder brand selection, demo architecture, language strategy and exact usability outcomes remain pending validation.

Proceed to Round 4 — Acquisition experiments and lawful lead intelligence. That round should connect the new buyer experience to a realistic prospect-discovery process, verified website diagnostics, permission-compliant outreach and measurable movement toward the first three paying customers.
