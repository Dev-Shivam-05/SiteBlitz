Google’s announced January 2027 removal of third-party “Send as” makes the proposed Gmail-web-plus-external-SMTP workflow a poor new investment.

Forwarding into Gmail remains supported. Accessing Gmail through an external client also remains supported. Adding a complete third-party mailbox separately in the Gmail mobile app is a different feature and remains supported. Official announcement and exceptions. 
support.google.com

Second, authentication is more than an app password

A personal Gmail app password authenticates access to that Gmail account. It does not by itself establish permission for Google to DKIM-sign as siteblitz.in.

For DMARC to pass, at least one of the following must hold:

SPF passes and its authenticated envelope-sender domain aligns with the visible From domain; or
DKIM passes and the signing domain aligns with the visible From domain.

Both are desirable; both are not mandatory for a DMARC pass. Simply adding Google to SPF does not force the SMTP server to use an aligned envelope sender. Likewise, p=none does not turn failed authentication into a pass. Google’s DMARC documentation. 
Google Workspace Help

Therefore, I would not approve personal smtp.gmail.com plus an app password as a dependable custom-domain sending design without actual aligned-header evidence.
