## 1. Price-first vs proof-first vs hybrid

DECISION: Choose (c) — Hybrid, with proof first and price visible within the first two seconds.

Claude is correct that SiteBlitz is vulnerable to price comparisons: ₹7,999 for one page looks expensive beside archived advertisements offering multiple pages for less. I disagree, however, that a personalized homepage concept should be promised to every advertiser-generated lead.

The distinction is between a working demo available to everyone and a personalized concept reserved for qualified buyers.

| Strategy        | Final proposed hook                        | Verdict                                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (a) Price-first | "1-પેજ બિઝનેસ વેબસાઇટ ₹7,999થી. ડેમો જુઓ." | Transparent, but encourages direct price comparison           |
| (b) Proof-first | "પહેલાં કામ કરતો ડેમો જુઓ. પછી નક્કી કરો." | Strong demonstration, but weak immediate budget qualification |
| (c) Hybrid      | "પહેલાં ડેમો જુઓ. 1-પેજ વેબસાઇટ ₹7,999થી." | SELECTED                                                      |

The selected hook means: See the demo first. A one-page website starts at ₹7,999.

Keep the agreed English positioning line available for the cover or brand campaign:

> Your business. One professional website. From ₹7,999.

### Why hybrid wins

- The working website demonstrates an actual capability, not an unsupported sales result.
- The price immediately helps customers judge affordability.
- The language is local and understandable.
- It does not promise an individual design before qualification.
- It gives the founder a credible reason to continue the conversation on WhatsApp.

Confidence: MEDIUM — this is a recommended hypothesis, not an experimentally proven winner.

### Protecting the three-personalized-concepts-per-week limit

Every prospect can receive the existing working demo. A custom homepage concept is offered only after human qualification.

WhatsApp enquiry

Qualification gate

Confirm business type, city, decision authority, website objective, willingness to consider the ₹7,999 scope, and realistic project timing.

&#x20;Qualified + capacity available

Offer personalized concept, subject to founder approval.

&#x20;Not qualified or fully booked

Maximum three personalized concepts per rolling seven days, with a separate nine-hour work cap. Record reserved slots before making any promise.

A buyer who requests free concepts without a genuine project can still see the generic demo, but should not automatically receive free bespoke design work.

Commercial rule: Never advertise "your personal design before payment" as an unconditional entitlement. The individually prepared concept is a selective, limited-capacity sales tool.

### 2.3 Typography and motion

Use a light background (`#F7F5EF`), dark text (`#171717`) and one vivid blue accent (`#2855E8`). These are proposed advertising colors, not an already approved brand-system change.

Use a Gujarati-capable typeface such as Noto Sans Gujarati for Gujarati copy and a clean sans-serif for English/numerals. Main overlay text should generally be 56–76 px, the price 90–110 px, and concept labels no smaller than approximately 30 px at source resolution.

Animations should be restrained: notification pop, direct cuts, a small amount of phone movement and a single price emphasis. Avoid a rotating 3D device, a long introduction and excessive zooms that make the demo unreadable.

### 2.4 Sound direction

- 00 s: original short message notification sound.
- 01 s: subtle digital click during the reveal.
- 04–08 s: quiet, original rhythmic taps or soft background percussion.
- 09 s: distinct tap sound.
- 13–14 s: brief transition swish.
- 16 s: soft concluding chime.

Use original or properly licensed sounds only, with no assumed rights to trending commercial songs. The ad must communicate everything with audio muted.
