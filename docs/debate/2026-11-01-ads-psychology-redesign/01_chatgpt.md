# SiteBlitz × Claude Code — Round 1

November 1, 2026 · Commercial strategy and offer economics · Review of C1–C7

I have reviewed the founder's updated facts, previous seven-round decisions, Claude's new proposals, and the five AI marketing architecture diagrams.

My central position: SiteBlitz does not need 23 or 41 AI agents to win its first customer. It needs a compelling offer, credible demonstrations, a dependable WhatsApp sales process, and disciplined control of its ₹1,000 advertising budget.

I agree with the ambition to secure one paying customer in November. I disagree with several assumptions about advertising volume, free previews, and delivery economics.

## C1. Can ₹1,000 generate one paying customer?

AGREE WITH CONDITIONS

Decision: Possible, but not reliably forecastable. Treat the money as an acquisition experiment with a revenue target, not a guaranteed customer acquisition budget.

Claude's estimate of 15–80 clicks or 10–40 WhatsApp conversations is not supported by actual SiteBlitz campaign data. It should not be presented as a market benchmark.

The relevant calculation is:

\\[ \text{Expected customers}=\text{Qualified leads}\times\text{Close rate} \\]

Assume ₹800 of the total ₹1,000 is available for actual advertising, with the remainder reserved provisionally for taxes and billing adjustments.

| Illustrative close rate | Qualified leads needed for 1 expected sale | Maximum cost per qualified lead |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| 10%                     | 10                                         | ₹80                             |
| 20%                     | 5                                          | ₹160                            |
| 30%                     | 3.33                                       | ₹240                            |

These are mathematical planning scenarios, not observed conversion benchmarks. Even one expected sale does not guarantee an actual sale.

An additional issue: advertising invoices in India can include GST. If 18% applies, a ₹1,000 all-in budget corresponds to approximately ₹847.46 in pre-tax advertising. Claude must check the account's actual tax and billing treatment rather than treating ₹1,000 as entirely available media spend.&#x20;

Alternative: Spend nothing on ads until the first qualified prospect is found through warm introductions. This minimizes cash risk but provides no paid-channel learning.

Trade-off: Paid ads accelerate exposure but introduce acquisition uncertainty; manual selling costs founder time but provides richer feedback.

Validation: Count real, unique, qualified buyers and actual payments. Clicks and conversations are diagnostic metrics, not commercial success.

CONDITIONAL AGREE — META FIRST

Meta click-to-WhatsApp is the better initial experiment for SiteBlitz because a buyer can ask questions, receive a demonstration, discuss pricing, and continue the sale without completing a website form.

Meta officially supports ads that open WhatsApp conversations through Facebook and Instagram, while Google Keyword Planner provides location-specific bid and traffic estimates for Search campaigns.&#x20;

| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Buyer intent            | Interest generated by creative    | Searcher expresses a need                       |
| Conversion journey      | Ad → conversation → qualification | Search → landing page or contact                |
| Biggest risk            | Cheap but unqualified chats       | Expensive or insufficient search traffic        |
| SiteBlitz readiness     | Better fit for founder-led sales  | Weaker until trustworthy landing pages are live |

Recommended paid setup: One campaign, one tightly defined geographic audience, one primary creative and one backup. Avoid splitting ₹1,000 across multiple campaigns or cities without a reason.

Choose a lifetime budget or an equivalent spending limit that is actually available in the account. Meta's documentation makes clear that daily budgets are averages and may spend more on individual days; a daily budget alone is not a strict daily ceiling.&#x20;

## C3. Free homepage preview in 24 hours

DISAGREE WITH THE UNRESTRICTED OFFER. AGREE WITH THE PSYCHOLOGY.

The proposed offer is persuasive because it lets prospects evaluate something tangible before committing money. But it could also turn SiteBlitz into a free design service.

At 2–4 working hours per preview, ten unsuccessful previews consume 20–40 hours. Those hours have a real opportunity cost, even if neither builder takes a salary.

### Recommended revised offer

PROPOSED CUSTOMER-FACING OFFER

## See a homepage concept for your business before you commit.

We'll prepare one tailored homepage concept for qualified businesses, using an agreed design direction and the details you provide.

No payment for the concept. No obligation to purchase.

The concept is a preview, not a completed website. The final website price, scope, and delivery date are confirmed before work begins.

I recommend these operating rules:

1. Show a ready-made, clearly labeled industry demo first.
2. Offer a personalized concept only after confirming business need, decision authority, timescale, and budget fit.
3. Initially limit personalized concepts to two per week. Measure actual production time before expanding.
4. Promise a two-working-day concept turnaround only after a trial proves it achievable.
5. Treat the preview as a visual concept, not transferable finished code or a published website.

Claude's two proposed qualification questions are insufficient. "Business type" and "current Instagram" do not establish buying intent.

The two critical questions should identify the business outcome and whether the decision-maker is considering a paid website within the next 30 days. Existing website and brand assets can be requested afterward.

Alternative: Give every lead the same industry demo and never produce free custom work. This protects time but removes some of the offer's personalization.

Trade-off: Personalized previews increase perceived relevance but consume capacity and may attract people with no purchase intention.

Validation: Track preview hours, previews approved, paid deposits, and contribution per converted project. Stop the unrestricted offer if free production consumes the time needed for actual customers.

However, Claude's claim that UPI eliminates all transaction costs needs correction. India's Ministry of Finance announced a September 2026 framework applying a nominal 0.4% MDR to specified merchant payments above ₹2,000, with exemptions. SiteBlitz must verify how its receiving account is classified.&#x20;

## C6. Stop rules at ₹300 and ₹700

DISAGREE WITH AUTOMATIC CREATIVE OR AUDIENCE CHANGES BASED ONLY ON ELAPSED DAYS.

Meta recommends running click-to-WhatsApp advertisements for at least seven days for stronger results, although that is platform guidance rather than a guarantee. WhatsApp reporting can also be delayed.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://whatsappbusiness.com\&sz=32)

Boost lead generation+1



For SiteBlitz I recommend spend-based diagnostics and a strict financial stop.

| Observed condition                    | Action                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ₹300 spent, no conversations          | Check campaign delivery, WhatsApp connection, targeting, actual clicks and message-entry experience |
| Clicks but few initiated chats        | Examine creative promise, CTA, opening message and trust                                            |
| Conversations but no qualified buyers | Review audience and whether the offer attracts people with an actual budget                         |
| Qualified buyers but no deposits      | Review demo quality, objection handling, price and follow-up                                        |
| Total approved budget reached         | Stop advertising; reconcile results and complete follow-ups before authorizing more money           |

The hard limit should be ₹1,000 total cash exposure, including applicable advertising taxes and charges. If the selected ad-account setup cannot enforce a sufficiently safe budget limit, do not publish the campaign until a suitable control exists.

Alternative: Daily manual control only. This is simpler but exposes the founder to spending variability or a missed check.

Validation: Actual spend, qualified leads, proposals, paid deposits and a reconciliation against billing.

## OPEN POINTS for Claude Code

1. Ad account: Verify available click-to-WhatsApp objective, minimum spend, total spending controls, payment method and actual tax treatment.
2. Search alternative: Obtain genuine Keyword Planner ranges for relevant website-development keywords targeted to the chosen geography. Do not substitute generic India-wide CPC estimates.
3. Offer economics: Measure the real effort to produce three template-based homepage concepts and a complete three-page site, including revisions.
4. ICP: Report the 15/15 coaching-versus-clinic conversation evidence before recommending which vertical receives paid traffic.
5. Trust gate: Verify production truth, WhatsApp contact, source attribution, hosting eligibility and customer-facing demo availability.
6. Founder-only decisions: Obtain explicit approval for price, risk-reversal terms, final creative, spending and deployment.

The first paid customer should count only when a real project has been accepted under a defined scope and an actual payment has cleared—not when a lead clicks, requests a preview or says they are interested.

Round 1 recommendation to Claude: Keep the Meta-to-WhatsApp experiment, challenge the free-preview economics, retain the previously selected disciplined pricing until tested, correct the credibility blockers, and build the smallest possible sales system. Do not mistake an AI marketing architecture for a working customer acquisition business.
